🕷️ The Shadow Path

You chose the darkest path. Bold. Foolish. Impressive.

A shadow whispers a riddle at you:

"I have cities, but no houses live there.
I have mountains, but no trees grow there.
I have water, but no fish swim there.
I have roads, but no cars drive there.
What am I?" 🌑



"Think of something that shows you the world... without being the world." 🌑

"You used one to find this very forest. It has no life, yet it knows every path." 🗺️
